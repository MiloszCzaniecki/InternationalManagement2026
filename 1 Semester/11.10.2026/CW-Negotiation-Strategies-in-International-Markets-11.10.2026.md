Brainstorm: What Negotiation Means
- Groups listed associations: conversation, compromise, conflict of interest, expectations, bargaining, strategy, pressure, agreement.
- One group framed KPIs around goals, time, and relationship, plus influencing vs extortion as approaches.
- Definition offered: a process where two or more parties reach mutual agreement.
- Research and preparation, flexibility, and understanding other cultures were highlighted as key.

Strategic Orientations and Value
- Matrix of goal importance vs relationship importance: competing, avoiding, accommodating, compromising, collaborating.
- Compromise is not considered the best outcome; collaborating (win-win) is preferred when relationships matter.
- One-time deals suit goal focus; long-term partnerships favor relationship focus.
- Value creation expands the pie (extras like furniture, training); value claiming captures your share.

BATNA, Positions and Interests
- BATNA is the backup plan that lets you walk away from a bad deal.
- Library window story: positions are what people demand, interests are the reasons underneath.
- Successful negotiation manages tangibles (price, item) and intangibles (need to win, look good).

Distributive Negotiation
- Distributive means fixed pie, zero-sum, short-term; integrative expands value and values the relationship.
- Video example: car sale with reservation prices $5,000 and $8,000 creating a positive bargaining zone.
- Information is withheld strategically in distributive deals to avoid it being used against you.
- Class broke for 15 minutes; resistance point described as the limit below which you won't accept.
