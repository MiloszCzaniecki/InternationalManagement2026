Class session building a group definition of business (interaction, exchange, resources, mission vs vision, emotions, AI) before splitting into groups A, B, C to define data holistically.

Class Logistics
- Two people still missing from the WhatsApp group; person said not to worry about them
- Students asked to form project teams of 3 or 4 by end of lecture; going solo is allowed

Defining Business
- Elements listed: interaction between partners, environment, exchange, responsibility/CSR, legal, communication and negotiation
- Also: relationship building (CRM), money, data management, people, product plus service, resources, place, funding, digital, know-how
- Marketing can come before product, e.g. Kickstarter crowdfunding, often for online games

Mission vs Vision
- Mission is the inspiring why; vision is the how: numbers, steps, phases, planning
- Mission-only risks no plan or accountability; vision-only becomes KPI-driven with no bigger picture
- it is said that mission-based companies live longer, since employees stay for meaning, not just money

- Nestlé cited as tension between nutrition mission and selling water for money

Emotions, AI and Purpose
- Fully AI-driven businesses now exist, so people are no longer strictly required
- Emotions cause repeated human mistakes; rejecting a company's mission makes engagement and productivity drop
- Debate on whether university matters with free AI; person credited discipline, habits and shared ideas
